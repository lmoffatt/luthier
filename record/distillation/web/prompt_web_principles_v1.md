The attached file holds the ideas of Luciano Moffatt about a programming language he is designing,
Luthier, extracted from what he wrote and said and from his conversations with AI models, in time
order. Each line is `r<n> | date | who | the idea`; "model" means an AI said it.

Read the whole file before answering.

1. What are the rules and principles of this language? Give at most 20, the most important first.
2. What are the rules and principles of how this language is being built (the way of working)?
   Give at most 20, the most important first.

For each one, a single line in exactly this form:

`L<n>. <the principle in one sentence> | rows: r<n>, r<n>, ... | first said by: <who>, <date>`

using `L` for the first list and `M` for the second, and the rows it rests on. Nothing else: no
introduction, no commentary.
