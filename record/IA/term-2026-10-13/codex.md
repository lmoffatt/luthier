Changed the alpha comparison to recursive `partsAgree`, with free-name agreement globally and separately for each match arm. This deliberately uses the specification's structural decomposition; it is not an independent implementation of that decomposition. Data names now stay literal at the point where Data itself is compared, as AlphaPartsAgree requires. Binder bodies are compared after nominal swapping, as underBinder requires, rather than through the former paired-name environment.

**Conservative alpha limitation:** patterns with two or more variable occurrences must have identical literal patterns. Single-variable patterns still permit renaming. This choice is necessary for this implementation because the law's `renameAll` swaps successively, rather than renaming simultaneously. For example let A, B, C be matches on `Lit (IntConst 0)` with respective single arms `(PPair (PVar "x") (PVar "y"), Var "x")`, `(PPair (PVar "u") (PVar "v"), Var "u")`, `(PPair (PVar "y") (PVar "x"), Var "y")`. Ordinary alpha relates all three; the stated armsAgree accepts A/B and B/C but rejects A/C: swapping y with x and then x with y restores C's original body. Simply adopting partsAgree without a restriction would lose transitivity. The model therefore meets the stated Alpha laws but does not recognize all ordinary simultaneous renamings of multi-variable patterns. A simultaneous, capture-safe renameAll in the theory would permit removing this restriction; the theory was not edited.

Manual Alpha audit:

Fresh nominal swapping identifies single-binder scopes; literal Data names and multi-variable patterns restrict the relation rather than equating distinct constants.
- FreeVariablesAreNames: Var comparison is exact String equality.
- RenameBound: Lam, Pi, Sigma and Subtype have unchanged annotations; the reverse swap in underBinder restores the original body, and freshness discharges its guard. The single PVar match arm likewise swaps its body back; its scrutinee is unchanged and per-arm free variables agree.
- AlphaKeepsHead and AlphaPartsAgree: explicit recursive structural decomposition rejects different heads, constants, labels, ordered fields and non-binding names.
- AlphaKeepsFree: the explicit sameNames check.
- AlphaCongruent: same-headed non-binding constructors recurse on the given alpha-equal parts; equal binder names take underBinder's first branch. The PWild arm has no pattern variables and its free-name check follows from the given body pair.

Replaced the model named `reduce` and its fuel helper with `stepper : Reduction alpha substitute := { step := stepTerm }`. Internal Done/Next becomes nothing/just at this boundary; there is no iteration or fuel in the model. The theory owns reduce. lookupBody ignores Declare and Datum while searching all acts for a Define. Application heads, match scrutinees and projection receivers step before root rules. Pattern inspection distinguishes Miss, Blocked and Demand; Blocked stops arm selection, including nested inspection, rather than selecting a later wildcard. An unresolved bare Var is never a nullary constructor. Nonempty free-name constructor spines remain admissible as Iota explicitly requires; declared or defined heads cannot bypass lookup. Repeated variables are rejected by name-linearity, without comparing their values. Manual Reduction audit (each result is one transition, never agreement through fuel exhaustion):

- Beta: a normal Lam head steps directly to substTerm x arg body.
- Delta: the first matching Define supplies the body, including after a matching Declare. Recursive definitions still produce a transition even when the returned term is syntactically identical.
- HeadFirst: a Next head, scrutinee or receiver is rebuilt in its surrounding App, Match or Proj; root inspection waits for Done.
- HolesStay: declaration-only Var and its application are Done. PLit inspection of that Var is Blocked and cannot reach the later wildcard. A first PWild selects its body. Hole and Axiom are Done.
- Iota: injection payload, free-name unary constructor argument and literal-variable cases bind the exact raw term; matching literals and wildcards select the body. Single staged substitution is capture-avoiding and alpha-equal to substTerm. Mismatched known introductions are Miss, while neutral holes are Blocked.
- IotaPair: both bindings are staged before insertion. In the first order the NotFreeIn guard makes the law's sequential substitutions equal to simultaneous substitution; in the mirror order the identity substitution for f.y leaves the other replacement intact. Fresh temporaries prevent either replacement from being rewritten by another binding.
- IotaSkips: distinct injection labels give a definite Miss, then the second arm binds y to v; equal labels satisfy the law's guard.
- ProjOfRecord: a RecordValue does not step; distinct field names select their respective raw values, including reducible values, without a second transition.
- Prim: integer +, *, -, <, ==; FloatConst +; StringConst == dispatch on the two literal operands. Results are exact Lit terms or Var "true"/"false", even when those names have definitions in the program. Var "+" itself has no built-in arithmetic rule: it unfolds only if defined, otherwise remains a hole.
- ExternIsValue: Extern falls through to Done. Applied host calls remain outside the stated laws and this model.
- ValuesStay: all listed lambda, literal, introduction and type-former cases, including Pair, Inj, RecordValue and Primitive, fall through to Done; their components are not evaluated.
- DatumIsInert: both environment queries (definition lookup and free-name checking) ignore Datum; all recursive step and pattern paths therefore give structurally identical results with an extra datum.
