**Conservative alpha limitation:** patterns with two or more variable occurrences must have identical literal patterns. Single-variable patterns still permit renaming. This choice is necessary for this implementation because the law's `renameAll` swaps successively, rather than renaming simultaneously. For example let A, B, C be matches on `Lit (IntConst 0)` with respective single arms `(PPair (PVar "x") (PVar "y"), Var "x")`, `(PPair (PVar "u") (PVar "v"), Var "u")`, `(PPair (PVar "y") (PVar "x"), Var "y")`. Ordinary alpha relates all three; the stated armsAgree accepts A/B and B/C but rejects A/C: swapping y with x and then x with y restores C's original body. Simply adopting partsAgree without a restriction would lose transitivity. The model therefore meets the stated Alpha laws but does not recognize all ordinary simultaneous renamings of multi-variable patterns. A simultaneous, capture-safe renameAll in the theory would permit removing this restriction; the theory was not edited.
