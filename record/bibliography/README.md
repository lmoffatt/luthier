## What each supports

Executing a program:

- `mccarthy1960`: `eval` and `apply` over the program as data, the origin of Lisp. `abelson1996`
  §4.1 writes it as the metacircular evaluator.
- `church1936`: the order of reductions does not change the result (confluence).
- `plotkin1981`: structural operational semantics, one rule per form, a step `t → t'`.
- `landin1964` (SECD), `felleisen1986` (CEK), `krivine2007` (call by name, weak head reduction;
  the machine was described earlier and unpublished): abstract machines whose state is a datum.
- `launchbury1993`: call by need with sharing, a heap that memoizes delayed evaluations.
- `moggi1991`: computations as monads, the ground of Haskell's `IO` and `do`.
- `carette2009`: one object term, several interpreters, in the final encoding, which the paper
  contrasts with the initial one (the term as a data type and each interpreter a fold over it).

Laws valued in something other than `Bool`:

- `lawvere1973`: `[0, ∞]` with `≥` as order, `+` as tensor and truncated subtraction as hom is a
  closed category; categories enriched in it are generalized metric spaces.
- `goguen1967`: sets valued in a lattice. `walters1981`, `hohle1992`: sheaves and valued sets as
  categories enriched in a quantale or quantaloid.
- `hott2013` §4.8: the universe as object classifier.
- `kozen1985`: a logic of probabilistic programs whose formulas take real values.
  `morgan1996`, `mciver2005`: predicates replaced by expectations, the weakest pre-expectation.
  `kaminski2016`: the same calculus for expected run-time.
- `mardare2016`: equations `s =ε t` indexed by a rational ε, quantitative equational theories.
- `green2007`: one query evaluated over any commutative semiring; the paper lists the tropical
  semiring `(N∞, min, +)` and a fuzzy one `([0,1], max, min)`.
- `bistarelli1997`: constraint satisfaction over a semiring: classical, fuzzy, weighted, partial,
  and probabilistic (§6.3).
- `fainekos2009`, `donze2010`: robustness of temporal logic, a real-valued degree of
  satisfaction. `annapureddy2011` (S-TaLiRo), `donze2010breach` (Breach): falsification by
  minimizing it.
- `loscher2017`: property-based testing whose generation is guided by a search strategy
  (simulated annealing) over a value the property returns; part of PropEr.
- `reed2010`: Fuzz, function types indexed by sensitivity `r ∈ R>0 ∪ {∞}`.
- `gordon2017`: effect quantales. `petricek2014`: coeffects. `mycroft2016`: effects valued in an
  algebra, graded monads. `orchard2019` (Granule): graded modal types over any semiring.
  `atkey2018`: quantitative type theory over a semiring. `brady2021`: Idris 2, multiplicities 0,
  1 and ω.

## Corrections to what the model said in chat

Checked against the sources above, 2026-10-09.

1. CPython folds `2 + 2` to the constant `4` at compile time. The sequence of loads and an add
   appears only with names, `a = x + y`, and the instruction is `BINARY_OP` since 3.11, not
   `BINARY_ADD` (checked with `dis` on Python 3.13.2).
2. Tagless final is the final encoding. The term as a data type with each evaluator a fold over
   it is the initial encoding; `carette2009` contrasts the two. The chat named them as one.
3. `mardare2016` is titled "Quantitative algebraic reasoning"; "quantitative equational theory"
   is its term inside, and ε is rational.
4. In `green2007` the `[0, 1]` semiring is the fuzzy one, `(max, min)`; probability enters through
   event tables, not as `([0, 1], ·)`. The tropical semiring is there as said.
5. `kaminski2016` has four authors, not two.
6. Coeffects are `petricek2014`; `mycroft2016` is about effects.
7. "Expectations" is the term of `morgan1996` and `mciver2005`; `kozen1985` gives formulas real
   values, without that name.
8. The S-TaLiRo first author is Annapureddy.
